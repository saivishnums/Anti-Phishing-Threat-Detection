Extraction of embedded OLE objects, malicious VBA macros, and malicious hyperlink inspection in PDF, EML, and Office documents.

Tracking Pixels in Phishing Emails:

Attackers often embed a tiny, invisible 1x1 pixel image inside a phishing email. When your email client loads the image, it sends a request back to the attacker's server. This confirms to the scammer that your email address is active, that you opened the message, and can even log your IP address and device type.
