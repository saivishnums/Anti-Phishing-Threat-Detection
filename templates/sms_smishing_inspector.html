SMS / Smishing Inspector

Natural language processing (NLP) intent classification and short-link domain reputation checking for SMS carrier spam and banking credential theft notifications.

Carrier SMS Smishing Mechanics:

Smishing attackers rely on urgency-driven SMS alerts (e.g., fraudulent bank freezes, parcel delivery failures, or KYC expiration) containing shortened obfuscated links. The module will analyze semantic intent and short-link redirection chains to prevent carrier-borne credential compromise.
